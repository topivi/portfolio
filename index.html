Background

A Journey of Passion, Code & People

My software journey began in the golden era of 8-bit home computers, writing assembly and BASIC on the Commodore 64. That curiosity about how computers tick at the metal level sparked a lifelong career in software engineering.

Over the past three decades, technology has dramatically evolved from monolithic on-premise systems to cloud-native microservices and intelligent platforms. Through it all, one fundamental truth remains: software is built by people, for people.

Today, as a Senior Manager at Fortum, I channel that technical depth into building high-performing, psychologically safe engineering teams. I specialize in bridging business needs with technical excellence, driving recruitment for future talent (including trainee initiatives), and mentoring developers through their career milestones.

"True leadership in software isn’t about dictating solutions—it’s about cultivating an environment where developers have the autonomy, clarity, and psychological safety to craft brilliant work."

Guiding Principles

How I Approach Tech & Leadership

01

Pragmatism Over Hype

Adopt technologies that solve genuine business problems and are maintainable long-term. Hype cycles come and go; sound design patterns and clean architectures endure.

02

Empowerment & Trust

Great developers thrive when given ownership and trust. Leadership is about setting the vision, removing obstacles, and stepping aside to let craftsmanship shine.

03

Mentorship as a Multiplier

Investing time in trainees and junior developers pays the highest compound interest. A rising tide of talent elevates the entire engineering organization.

04

Respect the Foundations

No matter how high our abstractions reach, understanding underlying system realities makes us better architects, debuggers, and problem solvers.
